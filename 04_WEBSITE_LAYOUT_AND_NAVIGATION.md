# PROMPT 04 — BUILD THE WEBSITE AROUND THE VIDEO

Use the existing repository and the actual video-derived sequence. Do not create a different concept or a competing website.

Build the shared site layout and functional navigation:
Home | Private Markets | Capital & Advisory | Insights | Watch & Learn | AccelRevenue | About | Contact

The homepage must be one coherent video-led scrollytelling experience. The video is the hero and primary visual identity. Use overlays and content chapters that appear in rhythm with the sequence.

Use a modern, darkish, premium editorial treatment derived from the footage's own colours and lighting. Do not invent an unrelated palette or visual world.

Build:
- Header/navigation
- Mobile navigation
- Hero with the Canvas sequence
- Scroll-linked text overlays
- Chapter transitions
- Content sections using the supplied business information
- Footer and contact pathways

Navigation must work. Do not add fake contact details or fake links. Keep typography readable and avoid covering important parts of the person in the footage with text.
