The Alpha LetterSample issue

Market pulse

Prices move daily; value moves slowly. Before reacting to a headline, ask what changed in the business, not just the chart.

Concept of the issue: margin of safety

Pay less than your estimate of value so that being wrong doesn’t ruin you. The gap is your protection, not your profit target.

Behaviour check

Loss aversion makes us sell winners early and hold losers long. Write your exit rules before you buy.
